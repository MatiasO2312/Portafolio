## 📂 Featured Projects

### 🏥 [Oncological Epidemiology Analysis — Ministry of Health TDF](https://github.com/MatiasO2312/Portafolio/tree/main/02_oncologia_ministerio_salud)

![Python](https://img.shields.io/badge/Python-3776AB?style=flat&logo=python&logoColor=white)
![Power BI](https://img.shields.io/badge/Power_BI-F2C811?style=flat&logo=powerbi&logoColor=black)
![OpenRefine](https://img.shields.io/badge/OpenRefine-3772FF?style=flat)
![Scrum](https://img.shields.io/badge/Scrum-6DB33F?style=flat)

End-to-end ETL pipeline and interactive BI dashboard built for the Epidemiology Division of the
Tierra del Fuego Ministry of Health. Processed 1,357 oncological records (2017–2025) across
17 variables, delivering a reusable toolset for regional cancer surveillance.

**Key contributions:**
- Designed and implemented a Python ETL pipeline that standardizes raw registry data into
  an analysis-ready format — reproducible on every annual update
- Built a custom ICD-O-3 morphology classifier mapping tumor codes into 12 clinical groups
- Tracked 54 transformation operations in OpenRefine to ensure full data lineage
- Delivered a 9-page Power BI dashboard with cross-filtering across establishment,
  sex, age group and diagnosis year — handed off with a user guide for autonomous operation

**Key finding:** 59.8% of cases have Laterality as "Not specified" — a data quality gap
with direct implications for 2026 epidemiological planning, now formally flagged to the Ministry.

> *Dataset not published — confidentiality agreement with the co-training organization (Sept 2025).*

### 🔬 [People Analytics: Predicting Voluntary Turnover](https://github.com/MatiasO2312/Portafolio/tree/main/01_people_analytics_turnover)

![Python](https://img.shields.io/badge/Python-3776AB?style=flat&logo=python&logoColor=white)
![Scikit-learn](https://img.shields.io/badge/Scikit--learn-F7931E?style=flat&logo=scikit-learn&logoColor=white)
![SQL](https://img.shields.io/badge/SQL-4479A1?style=flat&logo=postgresql&logoColor=white)
![Power BI](https://img.shields.io/badge/Power_BI-F2C811?style=flat&logo=powerbi&logoColor=black)

Classification model to identify flight-risk employees using engagement, tenure and
compensation data. Built on a synthetic HR dataset designed to mirror real organizational
behavior patterns studied in the organizational sociology literature.

**Key insight:** The 3 strongest predictors of turnover weren't salary —
they were manager feedback frequency, cross-team mobility and role clarity score.
